# Privacy Policy for BarProbe

**Last updated:** August 12, 2026

## Overview

BarProbe is an Android application for generating and scanning barcodes, developed for
functionality and security testing purposes. This policy explains what data the app
collects, stores, and transmits.

## Data Collection

**BarProbe does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or user
information.**

Specifically:

- **No account or registration** — the app does not require sign-in and has no concept
  of a user identity.
- **No analytics or telemetry** — the app does not include any analytics SDKs, crash
  reporters, or usage-tracking libraries.
- **No network transmission** — the app does not send data to any server, first-party or
  third-party. It functions entirely offline.
- **No advertising** — the app contains no ad networks or ad identifiers.

## Camera Access

BarProbe requests camera permission solely to scan barcodes on-device. Camera frames are
processed locally for barcode decoding and are never stored, recorded, or transmitted
anywhere.

## Local Storage

Barcodes you generate, scan, or catalogue are saved only in the app's local storage on
your device (e.g., your barcode libraries, exported images/PDFs). This data stays on
your device under your control and is never transmitted to the developer or any third
party. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

## Third-Party Services

BarProbe does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that collect
user data.

## Children's Privacy

Since BarProbe collects no data of any kind, it does not knowingly or unknowingly
collect information from children or any other users.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, an updated version will be posted here with a revised "Last
updated" date.
